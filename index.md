Hi! I’m Emma, a PhD candidate in biostatistics (minor in computer science) at Indiana University Indianapolis, expecting to graduate in August 2027. I work on clinical trial simulation, causal inference with real-world data, and drug safety.

## Research

- **Clinical trial simulation.** I built [EmpiricalSim](https://github.com/ychen-98/EmpiricalSim), an open-source R package that simulates patient-level trial data with survival and mixed-type endpoints from historical data and target summary statistics.
- **Causal inference with real-world data.** I study treatment effects and adverse outcomes using large health data sources, including All of Us, N3C, Optum and MarketScan. My current work develops a cross-sectional difference-in-differences method for surveys that measure each person only once.
- **Drug safety.** I work on methods for detecting adverse drug events, including signals that appear only in specific patient subpopulations.
